# The Mentalist
is an acclaimed American crime drama and police procedural television series that originally aired on CBS from September 23, 2008, to February 18, 2015, spanning 7 seasons and 151 episodes.

Created by Bruno Heller, the show combines an episodic "case-of-the-week" structure with a dark, overarching revenge.

## Core Premise
The story follows Patrick Jane (played by Simon Baker), a former celebrity "psychic" who made a lucrative living using psychological manipulation, keen observation, and cold reading. His life shatters when he publicly mocks a notorious serial killer known as Red John.

Grief-stricken and harboring an intense desire for vengeance, Jane abandons his fraudulent psychic persona. He joins the California Bureau of Investigation (CBI) as an independent consultant. While helping a serious-crimes unit solve homicides, he uses his official position to quietly track down his family's killer.

### Key Characters & Cast
-  **Patrick Jane (Simon Baker):** The charismatic, tea-drinking protagonist. He lacks physical combat skills and dislikes guns, instead utilizing hypnosis, misdirection, and psychological chess matches to trick criminals into exposing themselves
-  **Teresa Lisbon (Robin Tunney):** The no-nonsense Senior Agent and leader of the unit. While she is frequently exasperated by Jane's complete disregard for police protocol and standard boundaries, she respects his brilliant mind and acts as his emotional anchor
-  **Kimball Cho (Tim Kang):** A stoic, deadpan, and fiercely loyal agent known for his blunt interrogations and unwavering focus on the law.
-  **Wayne Rigsby (Owain Yeoman):** The team's arson specialist, who provides muscle and has a soft-hearted nature.
-  **Grace Van Pelt (Amanda Righetti):** The rookie of the team, specializing in tech and research, whose deep-seated faith often contrasts with Jane’s cynicism.

-  **Acclaimed Performance:** Simon Baker received widespread critical praise, earning Primetime Emmy and Golden Globe nominations for his magnetic and deeply layered portrayal of Patrick Jane.
